ABOUT US

HARNESSING ENERGY, EMPOWERING EARTH

At Ecopower, we redefine sustainability by converting waste heat into clean, usable energy. Our innovative systems use thermoelectric generators and steam turbines to generate electricity from industrial and urban waste heat sources. With a mission to reduce pollution and drive energy efficiency, we are helping shape a smarter, greener future — one watt at a time.

Yes, EcoPower is designed with advanced safety systems to ensure residential and industrial safety.

What kind of waste can be used?

It can process biodegradable, plastic, and household waste with minimal preprocessing.
